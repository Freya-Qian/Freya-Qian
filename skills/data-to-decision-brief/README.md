# Data to Decision Brief

A structured workflow for checking business or product data, analyzing a defined question, and communicating findings in a decision-ready brief.

## What it does

- Inspects dataset quality, grain, filters, time windows, and transformations.
- Uses analysis methods appropriate to the question and data design.
- Separates observed results from interpretations and hypotheses.
- Produces traceable findings, caveats, and optional chart or presentation guidance.

## Use

Provide a CSV, Excel workbook, metrics extract, experiment results, or other accessible structured data together with the decision question and any metric definitions. The Skill checks what it can access and states data limitations before drawing conclusions.

## Contents

- `SKILL.md` — analysis workflow and guardrails.
- `references/brief-template.md` — reusable decision brief structure.

## Privacy

Do not add confidential datasets, customer records, credentials, or private business information to this public Skill package.
